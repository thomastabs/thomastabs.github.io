# Product

Inferred from the repository and from the owner's instructions while working on the site. Not yet confirmed in an Impeccable interview: treat "Open" items as undecided.

## Platform

web

## Purpose

Personal site of Tomás Taborda, a software engineer in Lisbon, at OpenSoft from November 2026. It shows who he is, what he has built and how to reach him. English and Portuguese.

## Users and jobs

- Recruiters and hiring engineers, often on a phone, deciding quickly whether his background is relevant.
- Engineers and researchers reading the Apex thesis case study, the dissertation and the notes.
- People who want to contact him about opportunities or software engineering ideas.

## What it shows

- Apex: his MSc thesis, a process framework for using AI agents across the software lifecycle, and the web app that implements it. This is the main proof of work.
- Other projects, experience, education and teaching.
- A notebook of written notes.

## Constraints

- No phone number or home address.
- No personal university data, such as citizen card number or individual course grades.
- Truthful content only: what can't be verified is left out or marked as pending, never invented.
- Thesis statement and abstract stay verbatim.
- Static site, deployed to GitHub Pages from `main`.
- Keyboard use, reduced motion and WCAG AA contrast are required in both themes.

## Voice

First person, plain and specific. Facts over claims. No sales language.

## Open

- The primary goal of the site now that he has a job.
